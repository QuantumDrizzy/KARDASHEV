# The series

Fifteen studies, one world (or one question) each, written to the same rule: every founding
assumption is stated, corrected where it is wrong, and every number that survives is derived in the
body or marked as an estimate. They are the content the [program](../program/) budgets the climb
from, and the database the KARDASHEV game runs on.

Read them in order: the Solar System Series takes a civilization from the inner planets to the rim,
the Interstellar Series takes it to the first stars and to the wells beyond, and GAEA turns the same
instruments back on home.

## The Solar System Series

| | world | the study, in one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [**ORPHEUS** — Venus](solar-system/01-ORPHEUS-venus/) | Venus cannot be terraformed *by* sound, but an acoustic control layer is a physically admissible control system for its three biggest problems — cloud opacity, volcanic stress, convective chaos — at ~10¹⁴–10¹⁵ W: a K ≈ 0.9 project on century timescales |
| 2 | [**TANTALUS** — Jupiter](solar-system/02-TANTALUS-jupiter/) | the cheapest He-3 mine in the system, because the ore is a gas you never lift: the fueling station of a K1–K1.5 civilization |
| 3 | [**KRAKEN** — Titan](solar-system/03-KRAKEN-titan/) | the only off-Earth world where a human walks outside with a mask and a coat instead of a pressure suit: the second home |
| 4 | [**INVICTUS** — Mercury](solar-system/04-INVICTUS-mercury/) | a Dyson *swarm* anchored on Mercury — statite films, hot electronics, radiators heavier than the collectors, factories that close their own periodic table |
| 5 | [**OKEANOS** — Europa & Enceladus](solar-system/05-OKEANOS-europa-enceladus/) | two opposite experiments at one price class: Enceladus vents its ocean into space for free, Europa holds twice Earth's ocean under 15–25 km of ice — and the decisive technology is a discrimination stack that tells life from chemistry without touching the sea |
| 6 | [**DEIMOS** — Mars](solar-system/06-DEIMOS-mars/) | not the 100-year terraform but the 10,000-year one: a civilization of moles below, the real economy on Phobos and Deimos, and the honest reason to go is insurance — a second biosphere |
| 7 | [**OURANOS** — the ice giants](solar-system/07-OURANOS-ice-giants/) | not failed Jupiters but the galaxy's norm: superionic ice inside, and the reference every exoplanet census is read against |
| 8 | [**CERES** — the main belt](solar-system/08-CERES-main-belt/) | not a failed planet but a distributed warehouse: the periodic-table tail of the swarm, the volatile valve, the market in the middle |
| 9 | [**HADES** — Pluto](solar-system/09-HADES-pluto/) | the demotion was a database decision; the census is the science — a living binary with a convecting nitrogen glacier at 44 K |
| 10 | [**OIKOUMENE** — the home system & the rim](solar-system/10-OIKOUMENE-home-system/) | what the nine volumes skipped: the Moon as the first rung where every doctrine was invented first, and Earth as the only completed project |

## The Interstellar Series

| | system | the study, in one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [**TOLIMAN** — α Centauri](interstellar/01-TOLIMAN-alpha-centauri/) | the only G2V + K1V pair within 15 light-years, with stable habitable-zone orbits that are empty: the first address where every doctrine applies at once |
| 2 | [**TRAPPIST-1**](interstellar/02-TRAPPIST-1/) | seven Earth-sized planets in a 7.6-Gyr resonance chain, being measured right now — and reading *bare rock*, which is a specification, not a defeat |
| 3 | [**ARGOS** — the Fermi question](interstellar/03-ARGOS-fermi/) | not a paradox but a boundary-value problem with seven quantified escape families; plus the Bracewell self-scan, the cheapest instrument in the program per bit of possible answer |
| 4 | [**TARTAROS** — black holes](interstellar/04-TARTAROS-black-holes/) | the galaxy already holds ~10⁸ Kerr batteries of ~5×10⁴⁷ J each; where the series stops being about worlds and becomes about wells — and how to operate the vault |

## How the pieces fit

```
series/    what each world can give, and what it costs          (the studies)
program/   the ladder K0.73 → K3 built from those budgets        (the plan)
src/lib/   the first-order physics every rung rests on           (the instrument)
the game   the same numbers, played: your decisions, their consequences
```

A number in a study that the program relies on is traced in [`program/AUDIT.md`](../program/AUDIT.md).
If you find one that does not hold, the [issue template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/number.yml) is for
exactly that.
