### 1.2 The tow (portable custody)

Operational constants: a 10 M☉ battery at 0.01c cruise needs decades of lead-time planning; **battery logistics is orbital mechanics with a ledger attached** — nothing about it is propulsion in the rocket sense.

## 2. ACCRETION-PLANT INDUSTRY (the regional power layer)

The tendable plant (TARTAROS §3.1) is an **X-ray-binary-shaped asset**: a BH + a donor, disk managed, jet captured. The operations:

1. **The feed (FM-10's disk diet):** the donor is scheduled — stellar wind harvesting or controlled Roche overflow; the disk's instability baselines (QPO classes) are monitored as plant-health telemetry against the wild-disk catalogs.
2. **The take-off:** power leaves as the collimated flow; terminal capture is the conversion plant. Jet-axis exclusion zones (FM-03) are zoning law: the plant's axis is the one direction nothing valuable ever sits.
3. **The siting census:** the natural inventory already exists — **the X-ray-binary catalog IS the list of pre-built plants** `[MEASURED: dozens known, census ongoing]`. K2.5 regional industry does not build plants from scratch; it *qualifies and tends* existing ones. The program's siting office is, operationally, a catalog-maintenance office.
4. **The efficiency ledger:** feed at 6–42 % conversion depending on BH spin — plant selection is spin-selection: the audit's most valuable column is the spin estimate of every catalogued BH.

## 4. RAMJET OPERATIONS (the ship doctrine)
