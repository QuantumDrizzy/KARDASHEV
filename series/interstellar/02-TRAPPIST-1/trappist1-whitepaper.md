## 1. THE CLOCKWORK CENSUS

### 1.1 The star is a planet-sized lamp

TRAPPIST-1 is barely bigger than Jupiter (0.119 R☉) and emits half a **percent** of the Sun's light — yet its HZ sits at 0.02–0.05 AU, where seven Earth-sized worlds orbit in days. Corollaries that matter:

1. **The system is compact beyond intuition:** all seven orbits fit inside Mercury's. Interplanetary Δv is lunar-transfer class, not Mars class — the seven benches are *close*.
2. **The whole sky of a terminator strip is dominated by the sibling planets:** from g, planet f is a crescent the apparent size our Moon *wishes* it was. Navigation by naked eye; navigation by clockwork.
3. **An M8's habitable zone overlaps the flare zone:** the HZ worlds are close enough that space-weather doctrine (TOLIMAN §2.2/DEIMOS bunkers) applies system-wide, moderated by the star's age (§3.4).

### 1.2 The resonance chain is the system's operating system

Period ratios ≈ 8:5, 5:3, 3:2, 3:2, 4:3, 3:2 — the same Laplace-style architecture as the Galilean moons, stable for 7.6 Gyr `[AGOL21]`. Three consequences the program banks on:

1. **Stability is demonstrated, not modeled:** 7.6 Gyr of the chain surviving is the strongest stability guarantee in the neighborhood — stronger than any N-body run we could make.
2. **The masses came from the resonance:** transit-timing variations turned the chain into an instrument. The system *measures itself*.
3. **The near-commensurabilities are a transport schedule:** resonance pumping (the Galilean-tour mechanism) means inter-bench hops at Δv budgets conventional rockets yawn at. The quarry has a rail network built into its ephemeris (§5.3).

## 3. THE WORLDS AS REAL ESTATE

### 3.1 The terminator doctrine, airless edition

All seven are tidally locked (periods of days at 0.01–0.06 AU). Without atmospheres there is no eyeball and no climate — but there is a *permanent thermal gradient*: dayside radiative equilibrium (~400–1,000 K for b–c, ~250–400 K class for the HZ benches `[EST]`), nightside cryotrap (~40–60 K), and a terminator band kilometers wide with every temperature in between, sorted and static. **Construction on an airless terminator needs no weather modeling at all** — just conduction management. The strip is the city; the gradient is the power plant (any heat engine straddling the line runs on the lock itself).

### 3.2 The quarry doctrine

Seven Fe-depleted (~21 wt%), volatile-rich-possible rocky bodies of graded size (0.33–1.37 M⊕), zero biosphere, zero atmospheric chemistry to fight: the manufacturing inventory is *pre-sorted by insolation*. Bench assignment by thermal role: inner benches (b, c) are the metallurgy furnaces (500 K ambient process heat), mid benches (d, e) the habitation strips, outer benches (f, g, h) the cold stores and radiator farms. The KRAKEN doctrine inverts cleanly: at TRAPPIST-1 the *ambient* is the cryostat for the outer benches and the oven for the inner ones.

### 3.3 The water-world question (f, g — open)

Agol-21 densities leave room for volatile-rich mantles on f/g (`[HYPOTHESIS: water mass fractions uncertain — degeneracy with composition]`). Two readings: (i) *ice mines* — volatiles are the boot's water/oxygen feedstock without a single import; (ii) *water worlds* — if f/g hold tens-of-% water mass, high-pressure ice floors seal the rock: no water–rock interface, no serpentinization chemistry, geologically quiet pools (the OKEANOS §2.1 chemistry needs the rock contact). Either reading serves manufacture; only the second is a habitability disappointment, and habitability was never this volume's claim.

### 3.4 The flare ledger, aged 7.6 Gyr

M8 dwarfs are worst in youth; TRAPPIST-1 has burned through its first Gyr-plus and still flares today (observed), at levels that matter for the close benches (b/c) and less for the HZ strip. The 7.6-Gyr age cuts both ways honestly: the great atmospheric-stripping era is likely *past* (whatever could be stripped, was — consistent with the airless census); what flares remain hit surfaces already vacuum-hardened. Bunker doctrine (TOLIMAN FM-03) applies at reduced intensity for the outer benches.

## 4. THE ENERGY LEDGER: THE K1.73 CEILING (the load-bearing number)

Full harvest of the star: L = 0.000553 L☉ × 3.828×10²⁶ W = **2.1×10²³ W**:

$$K_{max} = \frac{\log_{10}(2.1\times10^{23}) - 6}{10} = \mathbf{1.73}$$

**A colony at TRAPPIST-1 can never be Type II-local.** The savings-account economics of the Type III ladder, quantified at a real address:

- The *ceiling* is low, but the *price of entry* is the cheapest in the universe: a statite swarm at 0.01–0.03 AU around a Jupiter-sized star needs a collector sphere of **~1.6×10¹⁹ m² for full capture** (4πr², r = 0.02 AU) — **200× smaller than a solar-statite sphere** (INVICTUS §2.2: 4.2×10²² m²). Local-K1 costs ~10⁸ times less collector area per watt at the low end of the band... in absolute terms: K1 (10¹⁶ W) around TRAPPIST-1 needs ~4×10¹³ m² of collector (at 0.02 AU flux ≈ 250 W/m²: 10¹⁶/250 = 4×10¹³ m² = **40,000 km²** — the area of Switzerland, floating around a Jupiter-sized star). **The cheapest K1-real-estate in the neighborhood.**
- The *ceiling* bites at the top: growth past ~10²² W (K1.6) saturates the star. A TRAPPIST-1 colony is a *storage node and a cradle*, never an empire.
- Portfolio math: **~100 M8-class systems ≈ one K2.7-class portfolio.** The ladder's doctrine, now with two real addresses: TOLIMAN plants the power plants (K2-capable), TRAPPIST-class systems plant the granaries and cradles.

## 5. THE SEED: THE LONGEST HAUL

### 5.1 The transit and the braking asymmetry

40.66 ly at 0.15c = **271 years of cruise**. ### 5.2 Governance across four centuries

271 yr cruise + 160 yr boot ≈ **430 years** from departure decision to local industry — with 40.7-yr one-way light lag (81 years per conversation). The library-drift problem (Type III ladder §4.2) reaches its design extreme: the arriving seed's *constitution* will have forked from Earth's several times during transit. Doctrine: the seed carries the **method** (STANDARD.md's provenance contract, the honesty layer), not the conclusions — conclusions are re-derived on arrival, from the arrival's own measurements. It is the OKEANOS protocol at program scale: **carry the rules that make re-measurement trustworthy, not a cargo of answers.**

### 5.3 The rail network (arrival bonus)

The resonance chain gives the colony its transport: benches ride their commensurabilities; a hop from e to f (3:2) reuses the Galilean-tour mechanism — periodic, propellant-light windows on a fixed schedule that was stable before Earth had oxygen. **The colony's calendar is written in the ephemeris** — a transport network requiring zero maintenance for Gyr.
