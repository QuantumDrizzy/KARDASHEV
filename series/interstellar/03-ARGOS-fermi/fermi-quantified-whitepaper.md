## 1. THE ARITHMETIC OF THE SILENCE

The proper form of the question is not Drake's equation as a number machine but as a **dependency graph of visibility**:

$$N_{visible} = N_{civ} \times P_{visible} \times \frac{\Delta t_{overlap}}{T_{galaxy}}$$

The series' own numbers sharpen it uncomfortably:

- Wavefront: v_eff ≈ 0.02c → galaxy metered in 1–3 Myr (type3-ladder §4.1). Galaxy age: ~10⁴ Myr. **Expansion time is ~0.02 % of the available time.** If even one civilization in the galaxy's history chose the wavefront, the geometry says *here* — long ago.
- Visibility: our own INVICTUS shell would switch the solar system's IR signature ON mid-climb (K2 waste heat is *the* technosignature). A completed K3 is visible across the universe (Garrett's constraint). Expansion *at our power classes* is visible.
- Silence: zero confirmed anything (E2's haystack).

**The sharp statement:** our analysis makes the silence more paradoxical, not less — because our seed is fast, our shell is visible, and our timeline is short. The three surviving resolutions are therefore narrow and testable: **rare life (f_l ≈ 0), early us (the window is young), or quiet-below-K3 (everyone stops or hides before the IR-visible stages).** Everything in §2 serves one of these three.

## 5. VERDICT

1. **The silence is strange under our own physics** — our wavefront is fast, our shell is loud, and 10⁴ Myr is a long time for zero K3s in 100,000 galaxies. The paradox survived our arithmetic; that is the finding.
2. **It concentrates into three testable hypotheses:** rare life (OKEANOS's n=2), early us (the portfolio's time-wealth arithmetic), or quiet-below-K3 (every civilization stops or goes dark before the IR-loud stages — the filter acts *at the climb*).
3. **The series is the instrument.** The n=2 experiment, the B-index, the 2040 scoreboard, and the Listen census are the measuring devices for the three hypotheses — a coincidence only if the ladder was never about knowing where we stand.
4. **And the standing answer for the program is unchanged by any family:** build the climb *and* watch the sky. Every family of silence gives the same marching orders to a K0.73 civilization — because against "we are early," the only rational move is to be, when the others arrive, already here.
