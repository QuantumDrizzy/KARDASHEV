## 1. THE PARKING MODEL (where a watcher parks)

A Bracewell probe's job is long-duration watch with minimal energy. The orbit mechanics pick the parking spots, not the aliens:

| Site | Why park there | Survey status |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun–Earth L1/L2** | gravity-neutral, full-disk view, thermal stability | L2 is *crowded* (JWST etc.): any addition is catalogued fast — low prior |
| **Earth–Moon L1/L2, lunar far side** | the classic (LOS radio-quiet zone; hidden from Earth's radio noise; sees Earth) | **never systematically surveyed at probe-scale sensitivity** |
| **Earth co-orbitals** (2010 TK7, 2020 XL5 — confirmed Earth trojans) | rides Earth's orbit forever, zero station-keeping | photometrically found; **spectroscopically unexamined in depth** |
| **Mars trojans / inner-belt resonances** | long-duration system watch | Lucy-class flybys sparse |
| **Jupiter trojans** (two swarms) | the system's long-term watchtower | Lucy arriving 2027+ — first real census incoming |
| **Kuiper belt, ~50 AU** | the long watch, hard to reach | beyond near-term reach `[KNOWN_LIMIT]` |

The survey doctrine: **prioritize sites where a *small active object* is thermally or radar-distinguishable from a *cold passive rock*** — and where we can look repeatedly.

## 2. THE DETECTION PHYSICS (what a parked probe looks like against the rocks)

1. **Thermal:** a dormant probe equilibrates to ambient (invisible). An *active* one (instruments, beacon, computation) radiates waste heat — a 100-K-excess sub-km source at 1 AU is a JWST/MIRI-detectable anomaly against a 170-K asteroid background `[EST: sizing per object — the mission's first deliverable is the sensitivity table]`.
2. **Optical/spectroscopic:** machined surfaces are spectrally flat, strongly polarizing, and time-variable in glint; asteroids are red/black/ragged. Multi-color + polarization photometry separates "rock" from "workmanship" at survey scale.
3. **Radar:** planetary radar (Goldstone-heritage) characterizes 100-m-class objects at lunar distances today; an L4/L5 artificial body gives a *specular, rotating-with-period* return no asteroid matches.
4. **Occultation timing** (LSST-era): sharp-edged occultation profiles = geometric/manufactured shapes.

**The falsification-first rule (house style):** every candidate gets its forgery family first — young metal-rich asteroids, RV debris, dead stages of our own rockets (the survey will find OUR garbage: the catalogue of human artifacts in cislunar space is itself a deliverable — you cannot claim an anomaly without owning the catalogue).

## 3. THE MISSION ARCHITECTURE (three instruments, no flagship)

| Instrument | Platform | Task |
|---|---|---|
| **SS-1: Photometric sweep** | LSST/Rubin piggyback + targeted deep fields on L4/L5, co-orbitals, lunar L-points | find "too clean" objects: flat spectra, glint, non-natural lightcurves |
| **SS-2: Radar/thermal follow-up** | existing planetary radar time + JWST disbursement | character to workmanship-vs-rock; thermal anomaly census |
| **SS-3: Cislunar custodian smallsat** | a smallsat patrolling Earth-Moon L-points and far-side | continuous cataloguing — of *ours* first (the ownership map), anomalies second |

The honest expectation: **SS-1/SS-2 likely find rocks and our own debris** — and that is the mission working: the first systematic ownership map of cislunar space is a planetary-defense and space-traffic deliverable *independent of the SETI answer* (the FRAGILITY doctrine: dual-use or it doesn't fly).

## 4. THE SYMMETRIC DOCTRINE (park like you'd want to be found)

If the program ever parks seeds (TOLIMAN/TRAPPIST era), this survey is the mirror: a civilization that watches parking lots will find ours. Two rules follow, written now:

1. **Transparency-by-design:** our parked assets broadcast a benign catalogue signal (identity, ownership era, non-hostility geometry) — the alternative is being catalogued by someone else as an anomaly.
2. **The expected-contact ledger:** every parked asset carries the STANDARD's provenance contract and a *first-contact payload* (the library, OKEANOS-Protocol-guarded). A probe that might be found in 10⁴ years carries the same cargo as one found tomorrow: the method, not the conclusions.
