## 2. THE MARKET (the location-value theorem)

### 2.1 What sells, and to whom

| Product | Seller | Buyer | Why it beats alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scarce elements** (B, P, Ga, As, PGMs, REEs) | M-type/C-type deposits | INVICTUS replication ledger | The dopant tail of vol. 4's closure problem: ~10¹² kg needed over the program — trivial mass, *nonexistent* in Mercury's crust |
| **Volatiles** (H₂O, NH₃, organics) | Ceres, C-types, Trojans | Outer-system programs (TANTALUS hives, KRAKEN domes, OKEANOS staging) | Ceres sits mid-chain; volatiles launched at 510 m/s escape are cheaper than hauling from Titan inward |
| **Bulk metal** | Psyche-class M-types | In-system construction | Only where local launch beats Mercury's mass-driver price `[VERIFY per-route]` — Mercury wins most routes; the belt wins *specific ores* |
| **Silicate/glass** | S-types | anywhere | Commodity; Mercury already supplies at scale |

### 2.2 The theorem

**Asteroid value = (scarcity × location-advantage), never mass × Earth-price.** The trillion-dollar memes price Psyche's iron at terrestrial metals-market rates — a category error the moment supply touches that market. The belt's real business model is *being in the middle of the traffic*: mid-chain volatiles, dopant tail, and service (the belt is where ships stop because *everything else is far*).

### 2.3 The INVICTUS tail, closed

INVICTUS §3.3 flagged the dopant gap as the program's true critical path. The resolution: **the belt supplies what Mercury lacks** — Psyche-class M-types for PGM/dopant ores, C-types for the volatiles Mercury never had. The ledger closes: Mercury (furnace + launcher) + belt (periodic table) + Ceres (volatiles + capital) = one supply chain, not two programs. The replication doubling rate (τ = 3 yr) was gated on this tail; the gate is the belt program's founding business case.

## 3. CERES: THE CAPITAL

### 3.1 The dead ocean world

DAWN's census `[2015–2018]`: ~25 % water ice by mass; Occator crater's bright deposits = **carbonate salts** (post-cryovolcanic brines); **organics at Ernutet**; Ahuna Mons — an active-geologically-recent cryovolcano; ammoniated phyllosilicates. Ceres is a body that had an ocean, salts, organics, and cryovolcanism — then froze in place. The belt's chemistry store, and its most defensible *capital*: infrastructure sits where the volatiles and the organics are.

### 3.2 The elevator (the capital's founding structure)

Ceres: g = 0.28 m/s², rotation 9.07 h → synchronous altitude ~1,190 km (center-referenced; ~720 km above the surface). A tether from surface past synchrony is **~1,000+ km with tension inside today's aramid/UHMWPE fibers** `[EST: standard elevator taper arithmetic — the only inner-system body where the elevator is legal materials-wise; Mars's is borderline (vol. 6 E6 was Phobos's, not Ceres's), Earth's is fantasy]`. Consequence: Ceres exports at *mass-driver prices without mass drivers* — the elevator is the fixed infrastructure that makes the capital a port.

### 3.3 The harbor doctrine

- Surface: mining towns, brine processing, the elevator anchor.
- Orbit: the *actual* city (Ceres orbit is where the logistics live — the elevator feeds it).
- The protection question: Ceres has (dead?) ocean chemistry and confirmed organics — **the OKEANOS protocol v2 applies at belt scale** `[VERIFY: Ernutet organics' provenance; any liquid remnant in the deep brine layer is a containment question, not a mining one]`. ## 5. BELT LOGISTICS: RESONANCES AS VALVES, TROJANS AS WAREHOUSES

### 5.1 The output valves

The Kirkwood gaps (3:1 at 2.5 AU, ν₆, 2:1) are not obstacles — they are the belt's *injection valves*: resonances scatter asteroids onto NEO-class orbits, which is where the meteorites come from. Program use: the same resonances are **free transport lanes** — place bulk cargo on resonance-chaotic trajectories and Jupiter's gravity delivers it (slow, propellant-free, centuries for some lanes; the swarm is patient — INVICTUS economics run on decades).

### 5.2 The Trojans (E7)

Two stable swarms at Jupiter's L4/L5, object-count comparable to the main belt, primordial composition (intercepted planetesimals from formation), visited first by Lucy (2027+ encounters). Program role: **forward warehouses at the most stable points in the system** — volatiles and rare chemistry parked *at the junction* of inner and outer system logistics. The belt's market has two branches: Ceres serves the inner system's tail; the Trojans serve the outer system's.
