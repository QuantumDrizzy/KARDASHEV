## 3. COMETS — The Volatile Tankers

- **The product:** H₂O, CO, CO₂, NH₃, organics, dust — the *entire* PERSEPHONE-class chemical inventory (vol. 1 §5.2), self-delivered to any orbit a tug cares to meet.
- **The physics:** 67P-class bodies (4.9×10⁹ m³, escape ~1 m/s) are *shovel economics* (vol. 6 §4.1's arithmetic at home); outgassing at 4–5 µbar near perihelion is a *free pumping system*; the dust-bunny structure (Ryugu/Bennu heritage: 30–40 % porosity) means mining is *collecting*, not drilling.
- **The doctrine:** comets are the outer system's fuel line — the tanker fleet the Titan/Jupiter/Triton programs draw from; short-period comets are periodic infrastructure (Encke-class: a scheduled delivery route).
- **The interstellar bonus:** 1I/'Oumuamua (2017), 2I/Borisov (2019) — interstellar objects transit the inner system *annually* by the statistics they imply. Every one is a free sample of another star's formation chemistry, caught mid-flight. The OKEANOS protocol applies; the sampler fleet is shared with the comet line.

## 4. CENTAURS & SEDNA — The Conveyor and the Sentinel

- **Centaurs:** the unstable middle (Jupiter↔Neptune-crossing), dwell times ~10⁶–10⁷ yr — the *conveyor belt* delivering Kuiper material inward. The belt-to-Kuiper logistics corridor, already running on gravity alone.
- **Sedna:** perihelion 76 AU, aphelion ~937 AU, period ~11,400 yr — the *inner-Oort sentinel*: a body that spends its year where the Sun is a bright star. Its orbit is the boundary marker of the empire's inner province; its origin (scattered-disc? captured? stellar-flyby? `[HYPOTHESIS: all three live]`) is the map's last open line.

## 5. THE OORT — The Empire's Rim

10¹²–10¹³ comet-class bodies, ~5 M⊕ of volatiles `[MEASURE: all estimates]`, reaching ~1 ly — the **Hill radius of the Sun's gravity**. Verdicts: (a) it is the system's true *territory* — a light-year of strategic depth no other star contests until Alpha Centauri's own rim; (b) it is untouchable until K2-era logistics (1-ly flight = decades even at 100 km/s); (c) its role in the series is *reserve*: the volatile fund of last resort, so far away it functions as a savings account rather than a warehouse. The rim is where the map ends — not because the objects stop, but because the *Sun's accounting* stops.
