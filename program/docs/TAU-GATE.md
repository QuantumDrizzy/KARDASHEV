- **Mass curve:** doubles at τ_mech (lunar/Mercury ISRU + lights-out manufacture) — the 10²¹ kg industrial base of INVICTUS rides this curve.

- **This is why ERAS put the crossover at 2075–2100** — the crossover is a *fab-tier* event, not a launch event. The calendar already accounted for it; this dossier names the mechanism.
