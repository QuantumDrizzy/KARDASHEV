## 4. K2.6 → K3: THE WAVEFRONT (the galaxy's arithmetic)

### 4.1 The colonization wave

Per-system cycle: arrive → boot (160 yr) → local-K2 (10²⁶ W at a new star) → launch next seeds (n seeds per system, targeting the next 5–10 ly ring). Effective wavefront speed:

$$v_{eff} \approx \frac{d_{hop}}{t_{boot} + t_{transit}} \approx \frac{7\ \mathrm{ly}}{160\ \mathrm{yr} + 230\ \mathrm{yr}(0.03c)} \approx 0.02c$$

Galaxy radius ~50,000 ly → **metered in ~1–3 Myr**. `[KNOWN_LIMIT: the range 0.01–0.1c effective spans the literature; the game exposes the dial — seed speed vs. seed mass vs. boot time is the endgame's core tradeoff]`

### 4.2 What a K3 is (and is not)

- **Is:** a statistical property — 10⁵+ systems each at local-K1/K2, the wavefront complete, ~10³⁶ W under management, a civilization whose *age* (2+ Myr) exceeds every institution that ever existed.
- **Is not:** governed. Light-lag across the galaxy is 10⁵ yr. **Governance radius decays with distance: a K3 is a population, not a polity.** The only shared artifact is the algorithm (the library) and its version control — the game's endgame meta is *library evolution*: 10⁵ independently-maintained copies of the ten volumes, drifting, occasionally reconciling (INVICTUS FM-05, galactic edition).
- **Time wealth:** a completed K3 inherits the red-dwarf era — 10¹²–10¹⁴ yr of M-dwarf stability. K3 is the first rung where the civilization outlives its star's class.
